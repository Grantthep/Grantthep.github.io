NatBook – booking marketplace for Bangkok

2026 · Full-stack web app

Shops sign up, add services and opening hours, and get their own booking page. Customers find a shop, pick a free time and book without an account. The owner sees new bookings on their dashboard straight away.

  • Double bookings are impossible: a PostgreSQL exclusion constraint rejects overlapping times, even when two people book at once.
  • Time slots are calculated per shop timezone, with tests for daylight-saving changes.
  • Customers can view or cancel through a private link; owners only ever see their own shop's data.
  • 16 automated tests run on every push. Deployed on Vercel, Render and Neon.
  • FastAPI

Self-learning customer support assistant

2026 · Issa Compass hackathon

An API that replies to visa customers in the casual style of real consultants, trained on sample chat history. An editor model compares each AI reply with what the consultant actually wrote and rewrites the prompt, so the assistant improves with every conversation.

  • Guard rails reject prompt edits that would delete most of the prompt or break its output format.
  • Every prompt change is versioned, with a diff view of what the model learned.
